My research interests are broad, and range from safety and injury prevention to socio-ecological systems and historical biogeography. I am a tinkerer by nature, am _incredibly_ curious, and have always enjoyed taking things apart to see how they work. Champion of open science and reproducible research, and serve that community as an Academic Editor at _[PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/)_, a Review Editor at _[Frontiers in Mammal Science](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/mammal-science)_, and as a moderator at _[SocArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv)_.

Academic background includes a BA in philosophy, BA in anthropology, MIS in GIS, and a PhD in anthropology. Walked away from a Master's in computational biology (all but thesis, or ABT), and working toward a second PhD in forestry. Am a Certified Ecologist (ESA), Registered Professional Archaeologist (RPA), veteran of the United States Marine Corps, and avid outdoorsman.

Husband; proud father; generalist.
